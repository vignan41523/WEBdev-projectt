HACKATHON PROJECT . 12 HOURS

ELDERCARE

A simple Android application designer to help elderly
people stay connected, remember important tasks and
reach for help when they need it.

THE STORY

WE DIDN'T KNOW
EVERYTHING

ELDERCARE was built during a 12 hour hackathon by
Vignan, Jaipal, and Teerth.

We didnt have strong knowledge of Android development
when we started. Instead of giving up, we learned while
building, experimented with Android Studio and used
ChatGPT to understand problems, write code and move
forward.

The goal isnt to build a perfect application
It was to understand a problem, attempt a solution
and learn as much as possible in limited time
we had.

WHAT IT SOLVES

01

REMINDERS

Helps elderly people remmember important
tasks and daily activities.

02

CONNECTION

Helps the stay connected with family
abd people around them.

03

EMERGENCY HELP

Makes it easier to reach someone when
help it needed.
